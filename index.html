About us

Axis Cooperation Ltd exists to spotlight talent and open tertiary doors.

Axis Cooperation Ltd equips young people, especially secondary-school students, with practical learning opportunities that grow curiosity, confidence, creative problem-solving, and innovation. We create project-based learning programmes, mentorship opportunities, innovation showcases, and school partnerships that help learners turn ideas into useful solutions for their communities.

Our purpose is to make education a pathway to possibility. We support schools to nurture future-ready learners, connect student ideas with mentors and institutions, and celebrate the talent emerging across Uganda through platforms such as Sunrise Innovation Magazine.

  • Education
    Provide S.1–S.6 learners with guidance, learning opportunities, and information that support tertiary education planning, career exploration, and inclusive STEM participation.
  • Technology & innovation
    Document and share student-developed projects in technology, agriculture, health, renewable energy, and related fields.
  • Partnership & growth
    Coordinate partnerships between secondary schools, higher-learning institutions, mentors, and industry partners to expand learning and progression opportunities.

A flagship project

Sunrise Innovation Magazine

Illuminating young minds, shaping the future.

The 2026–2027 academic-year edition is a national platform for student-led scientific, technological, and creative problem-solving. Printed and digital copies go to headteachers, career-guidance departments, science clubs, and school libraries.

Who can submit: secondary students (S.1–S.6), guided by a patron teacher, subject instructor, or administrator. There is no limit on the number of projects a school may send.

  • Project summary of 300–500 words (problem, materials, method, application)
  • High-resolution photographs or diagrams of the prototype and team
  • Student names and classes, plus the guiding teacher’s contacts
  • Signed school endorsement, and parental media consent for minors

Outstanding Student Innovation Scholarships

Featured projects can reach university partners.

Axis Cooperation has invited universities and tertiary institutions to consider exceptional student work published in the magazine for partial or full scholarships. The Secretariat reviews submissions for authenticity and educational value, then nominates top-tier projects to participating higher-education partners.

Scholarship nomination

Qualifying innovations are forwarded to partner universities for scholarship consideration — a direct reward for creative student talent.

Admissions & financial aid

Partner pages carry verified entry requirements, application processes, key dates, and contact channels.

Career guidance

Faculty and admissions officers contribute advice on emerging fields, technical pathways, and the move from secondary to campus.

Girl-child STEM

Dedicated features celebrate female student leadership in science and technology, in line with inclusive participation.

Partnership

Two tracks: secondary schools, then higher education.

Partnership is governed by Terms of Reference, a non-disclosure agreement, and an institutional application. Schools remain the source of student work. Universities, tertiary institutes, and scholarship bodies supply programmes, scholarships, and optional award sponsorship.

Secondary schools

Invitation to headteachers: join the Secondary Schools Participation & Innovation Network for the 2026–2027 edition.

  • Name a patron teacher as the institutional point of contact.
  • Verify that submissions are authentic student work.
  • Collect signed guardian media-release forms before sending photographs.
  • Meet the secretariat’s submission deadline set in the ToR.

Schools are encouraged to send gender-balanced teams. Plagiarism or fabricated results lead to disqualification.

Universities & tertiary institutions

The Higher Education & Scholarship Partnership lets institutions speak directly to science clubs, career masters, and parents.

  • Scholarship & Admission Spotlight — one full page.
  • Higher Education & Career Partner — two-page spread.
  • Category Lead & Awards Sponsor — multi-page branding on an innovation award (for example, Best Secondary ICT Project).

Institutions designate a coordinator, supply logos and copy, and sign off page proofs before print and digital release.
